# C.04.6 corpus resynchronisation

The team fixtures are regenerated from their fixed seeds after the C.04.6
corrections. Their declared pairings now use fixed team pairing numbers,
pairing-number bracket order, and standings-score colour allocation. The Baku
fixtures also use the correct match-point and game-point acceleration scores.

The regenerated records carry the downstream pairings and final standings that
follow from those corrections. This includes the EDEBT ranks: the engine was
already applying EDEBT correctly, but the old records had been generated with
an older vendored tie-break implementation and therefore declared stale ranks.

The corpus generator no longer injects record 299 as a random mutation. Record
299 has several distinct forms whose score semantics need focused fixtures;
mixing it into pairing and standings identities made unrelated generated cases
ambiguous. The dedicated record-299 regression tests remain responsible for
those forms.

Every team expectation previously classified as a known failure now agrees
with the engine. Incompletable rounds retain the existing article-3.3.3
fallback and remain valid.
