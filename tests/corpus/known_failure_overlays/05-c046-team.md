# C.04.6 corpus resynchronisation

The team fixtures are updated for the C.04.6 changes and are stored in
`fixture_overlays/05-c046-team.jsonl.gz`, which the harness applies on top of
the shared `corpus.jsonl.gz`. Their declared pairings use fixed team pairing
numbers, pairing-number bracket order, and standings-score colour allocation.
The Baku fixtures use the match-point and game-point acceleration scores.

The updated records carry the downstream pairings and final standings that
follow from those changes, including the EDEBT ranks.

The team fixtures no longer carry record 299. Record 299 has several distinct
forms whose score semantics need focused fixtures; mixing it into pairing and
standings identities made unrelated cases ambiguous. The dedicated record-299
regression tests remain responsible for those forms.

Every team expectation previously classified as a known failure now agrees
with the engine. Incompletable rounds retain the existing article-3.3.3
fallback and remain valid.
